# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to make a piece of software work. Be
useful to each other.

- Be respectful. Disagree about the code, not about the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear before concluding it
  is wrong.
- Keep to the topic of the issue or pull request.
- Credit other people's work. Do not present someone else's change as your own.
- No harassment, threats, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Do not ask for, offer, or share game files or other proprietary material
  here, in any form. See `docs/asset-policy.md`.

Comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules may be edited,
hidden or closed, and repeat offenders may be blocked. To report a problem
with someone's behaviour, contact the repository owner through GitHub, on
Telegram (`desertdev`), on Discord (`desert_gamer`) or by e-mail
(desert_gamer@icloud.com).
